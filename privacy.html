Privacy policy

Jarvis is used only by its owner. With the owner's consent it reads his Google Calendar, Contacts and Gmail, read-only.

The data is processed on the owner's own computers. Some text may be sent to Anthropic's Claude to generate replies. Camera images, location and children's chats are never sent to a cloud service.

Data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
